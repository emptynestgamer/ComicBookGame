About ComicBookGame!

What is this?

ComicBookGame! is a simulation game made for comic book collectors, by a collector. Buy, hold, and flip comics without spending a real dime.

Learn the hobby

Practice the art of identifying key issues, timing the market, and managing a collection, all within a risk-free sandbox environment.

Important note

This is a comic book collection simulator. Prices shown during eBay search reflect real active listings via the eBay API. All simulated market prices are fictional. ComicBookGame.com is an independent project and eBay affiliate. We are not affiliated with CGC, CBCS, or any grading company.

How to Play

Starting the Simulation

Enable Simulated Market from the footer to start the clock. Time moves in months. One tick every 20 seconds. The Log tab records everything that happens.

Cost of Living

Every month, $50.00 is deducted from your Cash for cost of living. If you can't cover it, the payment is skipped and you receive a strike. Three consecutive strikes and it's Game Over. Paying resets your strike count.

Work or Event

Each month you choose: Work to earn $60.00, or attend an Event, a local sale or convention where discounted comics are available to add to your portfolio. You can also enable Auto-Work in the footer to skip the choice and earn automatically.

The Market

While the simulation runs, comic prices fluctuate each tick with a slight upward bias mimicking long-term appreciation. Watch your portfolio value in the Dashboard and sell at the right moment for maximum profit.

Game Over

If you miss cost of living payments three months in a row, the game ends. Sell comics to stay liquid, work consistently, and don't let your Cash run dry.

Privacy Policy

Data storage

All game data is stored locally in your browser using localStorage. Nothing is sent to any server except when using the eBay search feature, which transmits your search query through our secure proxy to retrieve listing data. No personal information is ever collected or stored.

No tracking

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal or financial data. No cookies, no analytics, no accounts required. When you click through to an eBay listing, eBay's own privacy policy applies. ComicBookGame.com participates in the eBay Partner Network and may earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

Disclaimer

Simulated market prices are fictional and for entertainment purposes only. Real eBay listing prices are provided for reference and reflect live market data at time of search. Nothing on this site constitutes financial or investment advice.
